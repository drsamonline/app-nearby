Nearby

Find the people you came with, inside a venue — a mela, a mall, a stadium. Coarse proximity from anchor check-ins: no GPS, no coordinates, no location history.

Open Nearby

The LAN transport is not built. The demo runs the whole room on one device so the proximity logic can be seen and tested. A real link between phones over the venue network is named as absent rather than faked — a demo that pretended to do it would be a lie about the one thing this app exists for.

Status Built and working: rooms and members; anchor check-ins that replace the last one; three proximity tiers (nearest · same zone · far) from a hop count over a venue graph; meet-point pins with a direction and an age; a lost-and-found board; a quick broadcast.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: the LAN transport, and therefore real multi-device presence. Also no QR joining — the room is created on one device.

What it does

  • Puts members in a room and asks each to check in at a fixed anchor — a gate, a hall, a stall.
  • Works out how near someone is as one of three tiers, from how many anchors apart they are.
  • Drops a meet-point pin at an anchor, and shows each person a direction to it rather than a distance.
  • Carries a lost-and-found board and a quick “I'm at the main gate”.

What it will not do

  • Store a coordinate. There is no latitude, longitude, distance in metres or bearing anywhere in the engine.
  • Keep a location history. A check-in replaces the last one.
  • Use GPS or the internet. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.
  • Claim a transport it does not have. The LAN link is named as absent on the page and in the README.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA room or member with no name, a check-in at an anchor that does not exist, a pin not at an anchor, a broadcast with no words — each refused with the field named.
No networkNothing changes; there is no network code at all.
Storage full or unavailableNOT SAVED, and the room on screen is unchanged.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; never replaced with an empty room.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js.
